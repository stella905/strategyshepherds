# Strategy Shepherds website forms

This document defines the eight native website forms and their response fields. All forms use the Strategy Shepherds purple and gold theme and post through the secure Apps Script receiver.

## 1. Impact Story Audit

**Purpose:** A free self-assessment that identifies whether an organisation’s biggest gap is the case, the coalition or the agenda.

**Description:** Your organisation may be doing strong work while the story around it has become too small. This short audit helps you identify the gap currently weakening your funding case, community trust or sector position. You will receive a practical diagnosis and the clearest next step.

**Questions**

1. Full name — short answer — required
2. Work email — email — required
3. Organisation — short answer — required
4. Role — short answer — required
5. Organisation website — short answer — optional
6. Country or countries of operation — short answer — required
7. Sector — dropdown — required
   - Youth, education and livelihoods
   - Climate, food and energy
   - Health, gender and social equity
   - Foundation, intermediary or nonprofit network
   - Social enterprise or corporate foundation
   - Other
8. Our organisation can explain in one sentence what future it is helping to build — linear scale 1–5 — required
9. Funders can easily repeat why our organisation is distinct — linear scale 1–5 — required
10. Our strongest evidence is organised around the claims it proves — linear scale 1–5 — required
11. Leadership, fundraising, programme and communications teams tell the same institutional story — linear scale 1–5 — required
12. Communities, partners and allies have meaningful roles in carrying our mission — linear scale 1–5 — required
13. Our leaders are recognised publicly for what they know — linear scale 1–5 — required
14. Our organisation contributes a clear point of view to one sector conversation — linear scale 1–5 — required
15. What has changed in or around your organisation? — paragraph — optional
16. Which gap feels most urgent? — multiple choice — required
   - Make the Case
   - Build the Coalition
   - Shape the Agenda
   - I am not sure yet
17. May Strategy Shepherds email you your diagnosis and relevant resources? — multiple choice — required
   - Yes
   - No

**Confirmation:** Thank you. Your Impact Story Audit is complete. Strategy Shepherds will review your responses and share the most useful next step. Your organisation’s work is already creating the evidence. Now let’s build the story that can carry it.

**Questions**

    - Leadership and team alignment
    - Evidence and proof map
    - Stakeholder and coalition direction
    - Executive thought-leadership direction

**Confirmation:** Thank you. Your application has reached the Strategy Shepherds team. We will review the context you shared and respond with an honest view of fit, scope and the clearest next step.
