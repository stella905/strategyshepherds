Build a communications system your team can actually use.

The Communications Desk Sprint turns strategy into practical infrastructure: a system for listening, organising knowledge, using AI, building relationships, distributing ideas and learning what works.

The problem

Most communications functions have grown by accumulation.

A social calendar here. A media list there. A consultant’s strategy in another folder. Reports across Drive. Stakeholder relationships inside individual phones. AI used differently by everyone.

The result is activity without enough institutional memory. We bring the pieces into one connected operating system.

What we build

Seven parts of the Desk.

Every Sprint is shaped around the organisation, but the system usually covers the same seven capabilities.

01

Communications priorities

Connect communications to organisational outcomes and the decisions you need to influence.

02

Intelligence

Build a repeatable way to track developments, people and conversations shaping your field.

03

Evidence and stories

Create an accessible system for identifying, checking and organising the knowledge you can communicate.

04

AI workflows

Decide where AI increases capacity and where human judgement must remain central.

05

Human networks

Map and strengthen the people who can help your organisation’s ideas travel.

06

Distribution

Build repeatable pathways across owned, earned, shared, human and paid communication.

07

Learning

Understand what is working and feed those lessons back into the communications function.

  • Distribution workflow
