Modern Communication Readiness

Assess where your organisation needs to modernise.

Modern communications now depends on two connected capabilities. The wider workforce needs practical AI, communication and knowledge skills. The communications function needs a system that can retain intelligence, preserve organisational knowledge and work effectively with AI and flexible specialist capacity.

Assess the Communications Function

The future of communications is not simply more AI. It is better people plus better systems.

Strategy Shepherds uses these two diagnostics because an organisation can have an excellent communications team and a wider workforce that does not know how to use AI or manage knowledge well. It can also have enthusiastic AI users across the organisation while the communications function remains dependent on individual memory and reactive content production.

The two assessments help separate those needs so that the next investment is practical and targeted.
