Creator Day Africa

Open the doors. Let creators discover the story. Creator Day brings creators into organisations, industries, communities and places so more African work becomes discoverable through human voices.

How it works

Instead of giving creators a compulsory content brief, we give them access.

Creators meet the people behind the work, experience the environment, ask questions and follow what interests them. Commissioned content remains separately commissioned and paid.

Movement principles

Access without hidden obligations.

Creator Day is not an unpaid influencer campaign. It is an experiment in what becomes possible when organisations give storytellers genuine access.

No compulsory posting
No hidden content brief
No follower threshold
Editorial independence
Clear access and filming permissions
Fair payment for commissioned work
For organisations
