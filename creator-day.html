Creator Day Africa

Open the doors and let creators discover the story.

Africa is full of organisations, industries, communities, businesses, places and ideas that remain invisible outside the people who already know them. Creator Day brings creators into those worlds so they can experience the people and work behind them, ask questions and follow what genuinely interests them.

People trust people.

Organisations increasingly need ways to communicate beyond their own channels. Creators, employees, experts, communities and other human voices shape what people notice and understand. Creator Day is Strategy Shepherds’ practical experiment in building those relationships through access rather than compulsory content.

A participating organisation opens its doors to a curated group of creators. They meet the people behind the work, experience the environment and decide what they find interesting enough to explore.

The principles

Creator Day is not an unpaid influencer campaign.

The value comes from genuine access and curiosity. Organisations learn how outsiders experience their work, while creators discover industries and ideas they may never otherwise encounter.

Editorial independence

Creators are not given a hidden content brief and are not required to post simply because they attended. Their curiosity and editorial judgement remain central.

Clear permissions

Hosts make access, filming permissions and boundaries clear before the experience begins so that everyone understands what can and cannot be documented.

No follower threshold as the price of entry

Creator Day is interested in storytelling ability, curiosity and fit with the experience rather than treating follower count as the only measure of value.

Paid commissioned work

If a host wants specific content to be produced, that work is commissioned separately and paid fairly. Access is not a substitute for compensation.

For organisations

Creator Day can help an organisation introduce its work to new communities, build relationships with creators, discover unexpected story angles and learn more participatory approaches to communication.
