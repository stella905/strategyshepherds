Creator Day Africa

Open the doors. Let creators find the story.

Creator Day gives storytellers access to organisations, industries, communities and ideas they may never otherwise see. The goal is discovery, not forced content.

Host a Creator DayJoin as a Creator

People trust people.

Organisations need more ways to be understood beyond their own channels. Creator Day builds those relationships through access, curiosity and real human contact.

Creators meet the people behind the work, experience the environment and decide what is interesting enough to explore.

The rules

This is not an unpaid influencer campaign.

The value is genuine access. Commissioned work is separate and paid.

Editorial independence

No hidden brief. No compulsory posting. Creators keep their judgement.

Clear permissions

Hosts make access, filming and boundaries clear before the experience begins.

No follower threshold

Curiosity, storytelling ability and fit matter more than a single audience number.

Paid commissions

If a host wants specific content, that work is commissioned separately and paid fairly.
