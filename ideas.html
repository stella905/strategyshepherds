Ideas

Ideas for the next generation of communications.

Communications is being reshaped by AI, changing trust, leaner teams, creator led culture and a world in which organisations need to communicate increasingly complex ideas. Strategy Shepherds studies what these shifts mean for the way organisations work, manage knowledge and build influence.

What we are studying

The questions behind the work.

Our ideas practice documents what we are learning while building AI ready teams, modern communications functions and more human storytelling networks.

AI and modern work

How should organisations build practical AI literacy without reducing adoption to generic prompting? What communication and knowledge skills will become essential as AI becomes part of everyday work?

Knowledge and institutional memory

How can organisations capture what teams learn so that knowledge survives project cycles, staff changes and consultant handovers? What should a useful communications knowledge hub contain?

The future communications function

What should remain in house as teams become leaner? What can AI accelerate? How should communications leaders use consultants, creators and specialist talent without losing continuity?

Stories that move capital

How do stories influence funding, trust, partnerships, talent, community participation and agendas? What evidence helps organisations understand communication as infrastructure rather than output?

Human trust

How should institutions work with experts, employees, creators and communities as audiences increasingly discover ideas through people rather than only through organisational channels?

Data, AI and Internet Governance

Our first specialist research territory follows the organisations, policy debates, evidence and public conversations shaping Africa’s data, AI and internet governance ecosystem.

Important expertise only creates change when it can travel.
