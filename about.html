About Strategy Shepherds

We are building the communication capability modern African organisations need.

AI is changing how people research, communicate, collaborate and manage knowledge. Communications teams are getting leaner. Strategy Shepherds helps organisations prepare for both shifts.

Too much organisational knowledge disappears when people do.

The person who knows the history leaves. A consultant finishes. A programme changes hands. The relationships, context, lessons and working knowledge that made the team effective scatter across inboxes, folders and individual memory.

AI gives organisations an opportunity to change that. Not by generating more content, but by helping teams understand information, communicate what they know and preserve knowledge so someone else can use it.

That is the work Strategy Shepherds exists to do.

Our ambition

Build capability that compounds.

We want AI to strengthen African organisations without flattening context, judgement or human voice.

Mission

Build practical capability.

Build practical AI communication and knowledge capability across African workplaces so modern teams can work with more clarity, continuity and confidence.

An AI ready African workforce where institutional knowledge compounds, lean teams stay capable and expertise moves across people, projects and generations.

Founder story

Built from a problem I kept solving for myself.

For more than 12 years, founder Stella Njogo has worked across strategic communications, brand, digital behaviour and influence.

At Shujaaz Inc and later at Tiko, she kept building personal systems to hold research, evidence, audience insight, organisational context, messaging and communication workflows. Those systems helped her work better, but they revealed a bigger problem.

If the system only lives with one person, it is not an organisational system.

Strategy Shepherds grew from the belief that the personal systems good knowledge workers build for themselves can become shared team systems, and that AI gives organisations a powerful new way to make that possible.

Stella also brings practical training experience as Lead Trainer for Digify Africa in Kenya, alongside work translating research and complex programmes into communication that can influence funders, partners and public agendas.
