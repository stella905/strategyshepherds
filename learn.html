Your team does not need a tour of AI tools. It needs to know how to use AI well in the work it already does.

Most teams have experimented with ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or another AI tool. The harder question is what comes next.

How should a communications team use AI for research, internal updates, reports, executive communications, stakeholder briefs, media preparation, content and knowledge management — without compromising accuracy, confidentiality, brand judgement or human trust?

The Modern Communications Desk Lab is built around those real communications workflows.

The core programme

AI literacy first. Application immediately after.

The programme can be delivered as a half-day workshop, full-day team lab or three-session learning series. We customise examples to your organisation, audiences and communications workflows.

01

AI Literacy + Good Judgement

Understand what generative AI can and cannot do, how large language models behave, where errors come from, and how to work responsibly with confidentiality, bias, hallucinations, verification and human review.

02

AI for Internal Communications

Use AI to turn meetings, policies, reports and complex information into clearer staff updates, FAQs, leadership notes, presentations, action summaries and reusable organisational knowledge.

03

AI for External Communications + Brand

Apply AI to research, audience insight, story development, executive thought leadership, media preparation, report translation, social content and repurposing — while protecting brand voice and editorial judgement.

Applied communications workflows

We train on work your team actually needs to do.

Participants practise with realistic communications tasks rather than abstract demonstrations.

Research + Intelligence

Scan a topic, synthesise research, compare sources, identify emerging issues, prepare stakeholder briefs and turn information overload into useful signals.

Reports → Useful Communications

Translate long reports and technical evidence into key messages, executive summaries, donor or partner briefs, presentations, FAQs, social posts and creative briefs.

Executive + Leadership Comms

Prepare talking points, speeches, LinkedIn posts, meeting briefs, Q&As and thought-leadership drafts without flattening the leader's voice.

Internal Knowledge Flow

Summarise meetings, surface decisions, organise notes, create handover documents and make institutional knowledge easier to find and reuse.

Media + Stakeholder Preparation

Build journalist or stakeholder dossiers, anticipate questions, develop briefing notes, stress-test claims and prepare spokespeople more efficiently.

Brand + Content Systems

Develop repeatable prompts and review standards that help AI work within your positioning, tone, evidence and approval process rather than generating generic content.
