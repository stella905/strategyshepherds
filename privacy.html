Privacy

Website privacy notice

A plain-language summary of how Strategy Shepherds handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you complete an enquiry form or the Communications Desk Readiness Assessment, we collect the information you choose to submit, such as your name, email, organisation, role, thematic area and responses.

How we use it

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, provide assessment results, understand demand for Strategy Shepherds offers, improve our tools and — only where you opt in — send relevant updates.

Analytics and campaign information

The website may record basic source information such as the page or button you used and UTM campaign parameters so we can understand which communications are useful.

Storage

Website enquiries and assessment responses are stored in Strategy Shepherds’ Google Workspace systems. Access should be limited to people supporting Strategy Shepherds operations.

Your choices

You can choose not to opt in to updates. You can also contact Strategy Shepherds through the website enquiry form to request correction or deletion of information you previously submitted.

Before public launch: have this notice reviewed against the jurisdictions and tools you ultimately use, and add the organisation’s formal contact details.
