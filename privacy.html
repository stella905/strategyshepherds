Privacy

How we handle information you share with us.

Strategy Shepherds collects only the information needed to respond to enquiries, provide assessment results, understand interest in our services and improve our learning products.

Website enquiries

When you submit a website enquiry, we collect the details you choose to provide, including your name, email, organisation, role, contact information, thematic area, message and the page or call to action that led to the enquiry. This information is stored in the Strategy Shepherds lead workbook and used to respond to you.

Assessments

When you complete an assessment, we collect your contact details, your responses, your calculated score and the recommendations generated from those responses. The information helps us provide your result and understand the capability needs organisations are experiencing.

Email notifications

New enquiries and completed assessments generate an internal notification email so that Strategy Shepherds can respond promptly. Your information is not sold to advertisers.

Updates

If you choose to receive occasional updates, Strategy Shepherds may contact you about relevant learning resources, events or services. You can ask to stop receiving these updates at any time.

Questions

If you have a question about information you submitted through this website, contact Strategy Shepherds through the website enquiry form.
