AI Ready Teams

Build an AI ready workforce.

Give your people the skills to use AI for real work. We teach teams to understand information, communicate clearly, manage knowledge and collaborate with better judgement.

AI literacy is now a work skill.

Every team processes information. Every team communicates. Every team creates knowledge that someone else needs to find, understand or act on.

We teach people how to use AI inside that work. No coding. No endless tour of tools. Just practical capability they can use immediately.

What people learn

Five capabilities. One stronger workforce.

The programme builds a shared level of AI literacy, then applies it to the work people already do.

01

AI literacy and judgement

Know what AI does well, where it fails and what needs human review. Learn how context, verification and confidentiality shape good use.

02

Clearer communication

Use AI to improve emails, briefs, reports, proposals, presentations and updates. The aim is clarity, not more words.

03

Better knowledge handling

Turn meetings, research and project learning into useful knowledge. Make what the organisation knows easier to find and reuse.

04

Smarter collaboration

Use AI to prepare meetings, clarify decisions, improve handovers and reduce repetitive work across teams.

05

Visible expertise

Help leaders and specialists turn what they know into useful briefs, presentations, articles and thought leadership without losing voice or credibility.

We teach better knowledge work.

Tools will change. The durable skill is knowing how to frame a problem, give useful context, verify the answer and turn it into work that matters.

Delivery options

Start small. Scale when it works.

Choose a focused briefing, a practical team workshop or an organisation wide capability programme.

Enterprise AI Capability Programme

For larger organisations
