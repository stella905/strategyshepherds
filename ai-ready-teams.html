AI Ready Teams

Your team needs more than access to AI. They need to know how to work with it.

AI Ready Teams is Strategy Shepherds’ practical AI literacy and modern work programme for organisations. We help employees across functions use AI to understand information, communicate clearly, collaborate, handle organisational knowledge and make their expertise more useful.

Bring AI Ready Teams to Your OrganisationTake the AI Ready Teams Assessment

AI is becoming a communication skill because communication is everyone’s work.

Every department explains work, processes information, briefs people, documents projects and turns specialist knowledge into something another person can use. The opportunity is to help employees use AI to make that work clearer, faster and more reusable without outsourcing judgement.

We do not teach coding, data science or a long catalogue of tools. We focus on the part of AI adoption that touches almost everybody’s work: thinking, knowledge, communication and collaboration.

What we teach

Five practical capabilities for modern teams.

The curriculum can be adapted to the organisation, but the core programme develops a common baseline of AI literacy and then applies that literacy to the communication and knowledge work people already do.

01

AI literacy and good judgement

Participants learn what generative AI does well, where it fails, how useful context improves output, how to iterate, how hallucinations occur and why verification matters. The session also addresses confidentiality, sensitive information and the parts of work where accountability must remain human.

02

AI and everyday communication

Participants practise using AI to improve the communication they already produce, including emails, briefing notes, executive summaries, project updates, reports, proposals, presentations and talking points. The objective is not simply speed. It is clearer and more useful communication.

03

AI and knowledge handling

Teams learn how to turn meetings, research, projects and experience into knowledge that other people can find and use. This includes synthesis, project lessons, reusable notes, practical summaries, frequently asked questions, onboarding material and stronger institutional memory.

04

AI and collaboration

Participants learn to use AI around team processes rather than only as an individual writing assistant. They practise preparing meetings, creating decision briefs, clarifying actions, improving handovers and designing repeatable workflows for recurring tasks while keeping clear human review points.

05

AI and expertise

Organisations are full of people who know important things. We help employees use AI to turn that expertise into presentations, stakeholder briefs, conference contributions, internal learning notes, executive talking points and thought leadership while preserving accuracy, voice and credibility.

We teach better knowledge work.

AI tools will continue to change. The enduring skill is knowing how to frame a problem, give a machine useful context, interrogate information, verify what comes back, apply judgement and turn the result into useful work. That is the literacy modern teams need.

Delivery options

Choose the depth that matches your organisation.

The programme can begin with a leadership briefing or a shared all staff foundation, then move into applied team labs, internal champions and practical workflow development for organisations that want deeper adoption.

Leadership AI Briefing

A focused session for senior leaders who need a shared understanding of the opportunity, the limits of generative AI and the organisational choices required for responsible adoption.

AI Ready Teams Foundation

A practical half day programme that creates a common baseline across a team or organisation. It combines AI literacy with immediate communication and knowledge use cases employees can apply to their work.

Applied AI Ready Teams Lab

A full day hands on programme that uses real examples from the organisation so participants can practise research, synthesis, communication, knowledge capture and workflow design in the context of their own work.

AI Ready Teams Programme

A three session learning journey that gives participants time to practise between sessions, return with real questions and build habits around useful workflows instead of treating the training as a one off event.

Enterprise AI Capability Programme

For larger or multi country organisations, we can combine organisation wide AI literacy, function specific labs, an internal champions group, practical workflow development, a playbook and follow up learning. The goal is to build capability rather than simply record attendance.

For communications teams

The wider workforce programme can be complemented by the Modern Communications Desk Lab, which helps the communications function build the specialist intelligence, knowledge, AI workflows, flexible capacity and distribution system it needs to operate strategically.

For larger organisations

Build capability, not attendance.

Training hundreds of people once does not automatically change how they work. For larger organisations, we begin by aligning leadership, create a shared baseline, apply AI within different functions, develop internal champions, capture useful workflows and leave behind a practical playbook. A follow up session then helps the organisation see what people are actually using and where deeper support is worthwhile.
